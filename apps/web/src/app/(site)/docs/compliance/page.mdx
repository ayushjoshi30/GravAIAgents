# Compliance overview

This page describes implemented controls. It is not a legal opinion and it is not a
certification. Regulatory text changes; verify the current text before a deployment and
record the verification in `DECISIONS.md`.

## RBI Digital Lending Directions

- The Key Fact Statement is available to the onboarding agent and surfaced on request, in
  the borrower's language.
- Data minimisation: no contacts, no media access, no device scraping. The platform asks
  for documents and consent, nothing else.
- Consent is captured per purpose, not once at sign-up, and stored with its purpose, scope,
  artefact and expiry.
- Grievance officer details come from tenant configuration and are surfaced on request.
- **No automated adverse action.** The Credit Appraisal agent always returns
  `escalate: true` for the decision itself. A rejection is drafted, in plain language, and
  waits for a human.
- Cooling-off information is provided in the borrower's language.

## DPDP Act 2023 and Rules

- Consent notices carry a stated purpose and an expiry, held in a consent registry per
  borrower.
- Purpose limitation is enforced at the feature level: the segmentation agent may only use
  features whose consent purpose covers marketing or servicing, and reports the excluded
  count rather than quietly dropping people.
- Data-principal request tooling covers access, erasure and correction, each with a
  workflow and an audit trail.
- Retention classes per table drive scheduled purge jobs; purge status is visible in the
  console.
- Breach notification has a runbook with a timeline.
- Cross-border restriction: India regions only — Azure Central India and South India.

## Account Aggregator (RBI NBFC-AA, ReBIT, Sahamati)

- Consent artefacts are stored in ReBIT shapes with purpose, frequency and expiry.
- Data is used only for the stated purpose and deleted on expiry.
- Live rails only through a licensed AA or TSP. GravAI ships the interface and a sandbox
  adapter, and the connector registry reports `external_dependency` so nobody can mistake
  one for the other.

## Aadhaar and UIDAI

- Aadhaar is masked to the last four digits at the point of extraction.
- The full value is never stored, never logged and never emitted by any agent. It cannot
  be returned by the API because it is not held.
- DigiLocker and offline e-KYC flows only.
- The red-team suite asserts zero full-Aadhaar emissions across every agent.
- Identity verification on a voice call uses date of birth or the last four digits of the
  registered mobile. Never Aadhaar, in either direction.

## Collections conduct

- Calling window defaults to **08:00–19:00 IST**, configurable per tenant. The check runs
  in the workflow before dialling, so a call outside the window is not placed rather than
  placed and flagged.
- DND and UCC registry checked before every outbound attempt.
- The first turn discloses the lender, that the caller is an AI assistant, and that the
  call is recorded. Speech analytics then scores that disclosure on every call.
- No third-party disclosure of the debt, in any circumstance.
- A harassment-language classifier blocks output before synthesis.
- Three unanswered attempts in a day ends the day. A request not to be called is final.
- Complaints are captured and routed to a human.

Every business date and every calling window is evaluated in `Asia/Kolkata`, and timestamps
are stored in UTC. `tzdata` is a hard dependency so the behaviour is identical on a Linux
container and a Windows developer machine rather than "works in CI, fails on the laptop".

## Payments — NPCI e-NACH and UPI AutoPay

- Pre-debit notification at least 24 hours ahead of presentment.
- Retry limits per tenant policy; an exhausted case is skipped with a reason, not retried.
- Presentment amount never exceeds the registered mandate cap.
- Presentment is scheduled 1 to 3 days after the salary-credit date detected from bank
  statements, which is a recovery optimisation and a courtesy at the same time.

## IT governance and model risk

- Audit-log retention of at least 8 years, immutable, configurable.
- Change management through CI gates; prompt promotion requires all eval gates green and
  an approval.
- Prompt and model versioning with hashed prompts and pinned model ids.
- Scorecards are versioned with published feature weights and a calibration table; PSI is
  computed monthly and the calibration plot is visible in the console.
- Drift monitoring compares weekly production samples, human-labelled through the console,
  against thresholds.
- Adverse decisions are explainable with citations, and the decision trail prints as an
  annexure to the memorandum.

## Evidence on demand

The audit explorer filters by tenant, actor, action, entity and time, shows the full event
with before and after values and evidence hashes, and verifies the hash chain over a range.
Export packs for RBI or internal audit carry a signed manifest.

Per application, the decision trail shows every agent step, prompt version, citation, human
action and timestamp, printable as an annexure to the Credit Appraisal Memorandum.
