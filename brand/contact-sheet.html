Derived from the Graviton wordmark

GravAI brand sheet

Every letter is an outline lifted from GRAVITON. The G, R, A and V sit at their original coordinates; the second A and the I are the same glyphs translated along the baseline. Colour carries the meaning — GRAV is the platform, AI is the layer on top.

Primary wordmark

Light grounds. Blue #204887 from the source file, teal #0C6B5F.

Reverse

Dark grounds. GRAV goes white as the Graviton Light file does; the teal brightens to #41B8A6 rather than inverting, so AI does not become the least legible part of the name.
